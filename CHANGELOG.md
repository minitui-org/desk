## [Unreleased]

### Added
- The status line lists the running apps by the number `1`–`9` picks them with, the current one highlighted with its title, so you can see what's open and which number is which. The current app's title is cut to fit the others, and on a screen too narrow for their names only the current app shows. A click on another app's name switches to it.
- Reader (`tuireader`, spec §13.10), the third app made for Desk: it reads the Markdown and text files of a folder where they are, with Markdown formatted, tables in columns, to the pane's full width. One block is highlighted, `F7` shows a caret for selecting text with the keyboard, and `Ctrl+N` sends it to Notes, quoted under a note named after the file, with where it came from and room to write. Inside Desk that's a `launch` of the `notes` entry; outside it Reader runs `tuinotes` in the same terminal and comes back after. Reader remembers where you were in each file.
- `tuinotes --append <name> <text>` adds text to the end of a note, making it if needed, and opens it there to write. Reader uses it.
- Tasks (`tuitasks`, spec §13.9), the second app made for Desk: a to-do list with due dates in a todo.txt file under `~/.local/share/tuitasks/`, which other todo.txt tools can share. Tasks with due dates come first, with their time on the right, and you filter by typing. `Ctrl+N` adds, `Enter` edits, `Ctrl+D` marks done and `Ctrl+T` sets when it's due (`tomorrow 15:00`, `fri`, `+3d`). Every change touches only its own line, and a line changed elsewhere first is left alone. While it runs, a task falling due notifies once, with Done and Snooze 1 hour; in Desk these are notifications and the status line says what's overdue, and outside it they're toasts.
- Notes (`tuinotes`, spec §13.8), the first app made for Desk: Markdown notes in plain files under `~/.local/share/tuinotes/`, with a list you filter by typing and an editor, where `Tab` indents. It saves as you go, a second after you stop typing and whenever you switch notes, switch apps or quit, including when the terminal closes. A note changed on disk while you were editing it isn't overwritten: your text goes in a new note beside it. Inside Desk the status line shows the open note, and asking it to quit saves first, or stays if it can't. Its screen is the same in Desk and out of it.
- `tuidesk` speaks the protocol (spec §13.4). Native apps show their name and title in the status line and switcher, and their notifications follow the urgency table, with Do Not Disturb winning. A repeated id updates its entry in place. `Ctrl+N` is now a list: Enter on a notification with actions offers them, and the choice goes back to the app. A native app can open another app from `apps.json` while it's in front. `Ctrl+X` and `Ctrl+Q` ask native apps to quit first, so they can save or ask, and pressing the key again forces it. The socket is in `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/tuidesk`, readable only by you.
- `MiniTui.Desk.Protocol`: `DeskClient` and `WithDesk`, one call for a MiniTui app to join Desk. It sets a title, sends notifications and opens other apps, hears about focus, notification actions and requests to quit, and does nothing outside Desk. Spec §8 and §13.3.
- The spec for Phase 2, native apps (`docs/SPEC.md` §13).
- `tuidesk`, the shell (spec §12.3 to §12.7): one app on the screen and a status line, with `Ctrl+Space` as the only key taken from the app. A switcher and a launcher, apps that keep their screen and say how they ended when they fail, quiet notifications with Do Not Disturb on by default, and optional config files.
- `MiniTui.Vt`: `VtTerminal.Start(info, setup)` runs `setup` before any output is read, so a program that rings the bell or asks about its colors as it starts can't get ahead of the handlers.
- `MiniTui.Vt`: `PtyProcess` starts a program in a pseudo-terminal of its own, with its size, working directory and environment, and reads, writes, resizes, hangs up and kills it, and reports how it ended.
- `MiniTui.Vt`: `VtScreen` turns a program's output into MiniTui cells: colors and styles, cursor movement, scroll regions, insert and delete, the alternate screen, wide characters and combining marks, line wrap, resize, the title and input modes. Any bytes are safe to feed it, split anywhere.
- `MiniTui.Vt`: `VtInput` turns keys, mouse events, pastes and focus changes back into the bytes a program expects, in xterm's forms or kitty's, following the modes it set.
- `MiniTui.Vt`: `VtScreen` answers terminal queries through `IVtHost`: device attributes, status, cursor position, mode state, default colors and kitty flags. It reports the bell, clipboard writes and notifications. A program can't read the clipboard.
- `MiniTui.Vt`: `VtTerminal` runs a program and keeps its screen current on a thread of its own, and a program that stops reading can't block it. `TerminalViewBuilder` draws one in a `CellBuffer`, showing the last complete frame while a program draws the next (mode 2026, for up to 200 ms).
- An example that runs a shell in a pane of an ordinary MiniTui app, and `tools/key_probe.py`, which compares the bytes a program receives with bare tmux.

### Changed
- In Notes, Tasks and Reader the list has the keys rather than its filter line: `j` and `k` move, `/` starts a filter, Enter keeps it and Esc clears it. `l` edits a note or goes into a folder or file, and `h` goes up a folder or from Reader's page back to its list. `?` opens help in the lists. Desk's launcher and switcher still filter as you type. Needs MiniTui 2.5.0.
- The launcher's, switcher's and notifications list's footer sits below a rule joined to the frame, and draws each key bold in the title color and what it does as text, rather than all of it muted, so the keys stand out as keys and not as more rows.
- Tasks mutes done tasks and shows an overdue time in the error color (minitui#88, in MiniTui 2.4.0), in place of the `✓` before a done task. `Overdue` stays in the text.
- MiniTui 2.4.0, which fixes the three gaps Reader found (minitui#89 to #91): Reader's Markdown formatting moved into MiniTui as `Widget.Markdown`, which Reader now uses, and `F7` shows a caret for selecting text with the keyboard.
- MiniTui 2.3.0, which fixes the seven gaps Notes found (minitui#74 to #79 in 2.2.0, #85 in 2.3.0), and Notes and `tuidesk` use it in place of their workarounds. Notes saves on `Ctrl+C`, a hangup and `SIGTERM` through `App.OnQuitting`, autosaves from `TextAreaState.Changed`, and asks for names with MiniTui's prompt. The notes list and `tuidesk`'s launcher, switcher and notification lists are MiniTui's filter list, so they look like its command palette, with a line under the filter and the matched letters in bold. In Notes a click opens a note, a double-click edits it, a click in the editor moves the cursor, and Ctrl keys such as `Ctrl+W` edit the filter. `F1` shows the keys in Notes, where `?` is always typed.
- `Ctrl+Space` opens the launcher straight away, and `Tab` swaps it for the switcher, instead of a bar waiting for a second key. Commands moved into both lists, shown in a footer: `Ctrl+X` closes the app, `Ctrl+Q` quits, `Ctrl+N` shows notifications, `Ctrl+D` toggles Do Not Disturb, `Ctrl+S` sends `Ctrl+Space` to the app, and `F1` is help. `1`-`9` work while the filter is empty, and pressing `Ctrl+Space` again closes the list.
- MiniTui 2.1.1, where Ctrl+J reaches a hosted program as itself on terminals without a keyboard protocol.
